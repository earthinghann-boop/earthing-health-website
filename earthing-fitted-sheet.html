Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the earthing fitted sheet? +
Place the earthing fitted sheet on your mattress with the conductive side facing up. Connect the included grounding cord to the snap point on the sheet and plug it into a properly grounded wall outlet (or use a ground rod for outdoor use). Once connected, the silver fibers conduct Earth's electrons through your body as you sleep.
What materials are used in your earthing fitted sheets? +
We offer two premium material options: 95% Organic Cotton + 5% Silver Fiber for a soft, classic feel, and 95% Bamboo Fiber + 5% Silver Fiber for superior breathability and cooling. Both blends incorporate high-purity silver fibers that provide reliable, long-lasting conductivity.
Can I wash the earthing fitted sheet? +
Yes. Our earthing fitted sheets are fully machine washable. Use a gentle cycle with cold or warm water and mild detergent. Avoid bleach and fabric softeners. Air dry or tumble dry on low heat. The silver fibers maintain their conductivity through hundreds of wash cycles.

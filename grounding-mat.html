The Science

How Grounding Works

Grounding (also known as earthing) is the practice of reconnecting your body to the Earth's natural, subtle electrical energy. The Earth carries a negative charged electron field. When you make direct contact with the ground — or use a conductive grounding product connected to the Earth — these electrons can flow into your body, helping neutralize excess positive charges (free radicals) that accumulate from everyday exposure to electromagnetic fields and synthetic surfaces.

FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

How do I use the grounding mat? +
Place the mat where your body can make contact with it — under your desk, beside your bed, on your chair, or any area where you spend time. Connect the included grounding cord to the mat's connector point, then connect the other end to a properly grounded outlet or our grounding rod (inserted into bare earth). Follow the safety instructions provided with your kit.
Is the grounding mat safe to use? +
Yes. Our grounding cords include a built-in current-limiting resistor (100kΩ) as a safety measure. This limits current to safe levels even in the unlikely event of an electrical fault. Our products are tested and certified by third-party laboratories.
Can I wash the grounding mat? +
The mat can be surface cleaned with a damp cloth. For deeper cleaning, hand wash with mild detergent and air dry completely before use. Do not submerge the grounding connector. Avoid harsh chemicals or bleach. The silver fiber maintains conductivity even after repeated cleaning.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a grounding blanket?

A grounding blanket is a conductive bedding product designed to provide a grounding connection while resting or sleeping.

By integrating silver fiber into soft cotton fabric, the blanket combines comfortable sleep experience with conductive functionality for wellness-focused applications.

What material is your grounding blanket made of?

Our grounding blankets are made with 95% Cotton + 5% Silver Fiber.

The natural cotton provides softness, breathability and comfort, while the silver fiber creates stable conductivity for grounding performance.

What sizes of grounding blankets do you offer?

Our standard grounding blanket sizes are:

  • 124 × 150 cm
  • 137 × 150 cm
  • 150 × 207 cm

Custom sizes can also be developed according to your market requirements.

What colors are available for grounding blankets?

Our standard colors are:

  • Chocolate
  • Gray

Custom colors are available for wholesale and OEM projects depending on order quantity.

How does a grounding blanket work?

The silver fiber woven into the cotton fabric creates a conductive pathway.

When connected with a grounding cord and compatible grounding system, the blanket allows users to establish a connection with the grounding source while resting.

What are the benefits of using a grounding blanket?

Grounding blankets are designed for wellness-focused lifestyles and provide:

  1. Comfortable sleeping experience
  2. Soft skin contact
  3. Breathable fabric performance
  4. Convenient grounding solution
  5. Relaxation support during rest
Is the grounding blanket comfortable for daily use?

Yes.

The blanket uses a cotton-based fabric structure that provides a soft touch, good breathability and comfortable coverage for daily relaxation and sleep environments.

Can the grounding blanket be washed?

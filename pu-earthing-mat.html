FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about PU Earthing Mat products.

What is the difference between PU Earthing Mat and regular grounding sheets?

Regular grounding sheets are textile-based (usually silver fiber or carbon fiber fabric), designed primarily for bedding use. PU Earthing Mats use a PU leather surface with an embedded carbon fiber conductive layer, making them waterproof, wipe-clean, and suitable for desk, floor, yoga, and office environments where textile mats would not be practical. The PU leather finish also gives a more premium, modern aesthetic.

How does the grounding connection work?

Each PU Earthing Mat includes a grounding cord with a safety current-limiting resistor. The cord connects from the mat's grounding point to the grounding (earth) pin of any properly grounded electrical outlet. This creates an electrical pathway between your body (through the carbon fiber conductive layer) and the Earth's natural electron supply. The built-in current limiter provides an important safety feature.

Are the PU mats waterproof?

Yes. The PU leather surface is naturally water-resistant. You can wipe it clean with a damp cloth. The carbon fiber conductive layer is sealed within the mat structure, so the mat remains functional after light moisture exposure. However, do not fully submerge the mat in water or use it in standing water.

Can I customize the size and branding?

Yes. We offer full OEM and ODM services including custom sizes, colors, packaging, and branding/logo printing. MOQ varies by configuration. Contact us with your requirements for a customized quote.

Is the PU material safe for skin contact?

Yes. Our PU leather is carefully selected for body-safe use. The carbon fiber conductive grid is embedded within the mat and is not in direct contact with skin during normal use. The mats do not contain harmful chemicals and are designed for daily contact use.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which plug type do I need for my country?

We offer grounding plugs in multiple versions: EU, UK, IT, CH, ISR, and AU. Please select the appropriate plug type based on your country's socket standard. If you're unsure, our outlet checkers can help verify your outlet type before ordering.

Are the grounding accessories compatible with all grounding sheets?

Yes, our grounding cords and plugs are designed to be universal and compatible with most grounding products including sheets, mattress pads, mats, and pillow cases. The adjustable grounding cord works with any standard grounding product that uses a 4mm snap or direct connection.

How do I test if my grounding connection is working properly?

Use the Conductive Tester to check if your grounding product is conducting properly. Simply connect the tester to your grounding cord and plug setup, then follow the tester instructions. The Testing Pen can also be used for quick voltage detection to verify your outlet's grounding status.

What is the warranty on grounding accessories?

All our grounding accessories come with a standard quality guarantee. If you experience any manufacturing defects or issues within the warranty period, please contact us for a replacement. We also offer technical support to help resolve any connection or usage questions.

Can I order mixed quantities of different accessory types?

Yes, we offer flexible MOQ options and mixed orders. Whether you need a complete kit with all accessories or specific quantities of individual items, we can accommodate your requirements. Contact us for a customized quote based on your specific needs.

Silver Fiber Quality

Not all silver fiber is created equal. Silver content, plating method, and wash durability directly affect grounding performance. Cheap fibers lose conductivity after a few washes — leaving you with a regular sheet. Learn what to look for.

Grounding Cord Safety

The cord is the bridge between your body and the Earth — but a poorly designed cord can be a safety hazard. Built-in current-limiting resistors and proper insulation are non-negotiable. Learn why cord quality is the most overlooked safety feature.

How It Works

Grounding Technology Explained

Our grounding pillow cases use conductive silver fiber woven into natural cotton or bamboo fabric. The silver fiber creates a continuous conductive network from your skin to the grounding cord.

When connected to the grounding system via the built-in snap, body static charges are safely conducted to the Earth — just like walking barefoot on the ground.

Silver fiber's natural antibacterial properties also reduce bacterial growth between your face and the pillow, supporting cleaner, healthier sleep.

What is a grounding pillow case and how does it work?

A grounding pillow case is a bedding accessory made with conductive silver fiber woven into natural cotton or bamboo fabric. When connected to a grounding system via a cord plugged into a wall outlet, it allows the body's static charges to flow safely to the Earth — mimicking the effect of walking barefoot on the ground. Silver fiber creates a stable conductive network that transfers Earth's electrons directly to your body during sleep.

How do I wash and care for grounding pillow cases?

Grounding pillow cases can be machine washed in cold or warm water (max 40°C / 104°F) using a gentle cycle. Use mild detergent and avoid bleach or fabric softeners which can degrade the conductive silver fibers. Tumble dry on low heat or air dry. Do not iron directly on the snap connector. The antibacterial effectiveness is maintained through 150+ wash cycles in third-party testing.

Is grounding pillow case safe to use?

Yes, when used with a properly designed grounding system. Our pillow cases connect through a grounding cord with built-in current-limiting resistor for safety. The system only conducts very low-level DC current from the Earth — it does not carry mains electricity. All products are tested by third-party laboratories (ITS, GW, CNAS) for safety and performance.
